# Unbilled revenue and contract assets

## What it measures
Under Ind AS 115, revenue from contracts satisfied over time is recognised as work progresses, often measured by
costs incurred relative to total estimated costs. When recognised revenue exceeds what has been billed, the
difference sits on the balance sheet as unbilled revenue or a contract asset. It becomes a receivable only once the
contractual right to invoice arises, typically at a milestone.

## How to read it
- Unbilled revenue rests on management estimates of total contract cost and progress. Optimistic estimates bring
  revenue and margin forward.
- Compare unbilled revenue with billed receivables. A rising unbilled share means more revenue depends on future
  milestones and customer acceptance.
- In fixed-price IT contracts, unbilled revenue should convert within a few months; balances ageing beyond that
  suggest disputes or delays.
- In EPC contracts, look at unbilled revenue together with claims under arbitration and retention money.

## Red flags
- Unbilled revenue above about 40% of total customer dues and rising.
- Unbilled revenue growing faster than revenue, especially near a fund raise.
- Large contract assets on projects that are delayed or under dispute.
- Reversals of previously recognised revenue after changes in cost estimates.

## Benign explanations
- A large fixed-price contract in its early phase, before the first billing milestone.
- Contract terms that bill annually or at completion, disclosed in the revenue note.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Revenue from contracts with customers note (Ind AS 115): contract balances, movement in contract assets and
  liabilities, and remaining performance obligations.
- Other financial assets or other current assets notes, where unbilled revenue is often presented.
- Key audit matters, which frequently cover revenue recognised on fixed-price or percentage-of-completion contracts.

## Questions for management
- What proportion of unbilled revenue is expected to be billed within three months?
- Were estimates of total contract costs revised, and what was the effect on recognised revenue?
- Are any contract assets related to projects under dispute or arbitration?
