# Reading quarterly results under LODR Regulation 33

## What it measures
Listed companies file financial results every quarter under Regulation 33 of the SEBI Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements Regulations. The standard format shows, for standalone and consolidated results:
- the current quarter, the preceding quarter and the corresponding quarter of the previous year;
- year-to-date figures for the current and previous year;
- the previous full year's audited figures.

Results for the first three quarters are due within 45 days of quarter end and are subject to a limited review by
the statutory auditor; annual audited results are due within 60 days of year end. The half-year results also
include a statement of assets and liabilities and a cash flow statement.

## How to read it
- Compare with the same quarter last year to remove seasonality; use the previous quarter for momentum.
- Only half-year and annual filings include a balance sheet and cash flow statement, so working-capital and
  leverage analysis is limited in the first and third quarters.
- Read the notes: exceptional items, segment results, changes in accounting policy, and auditor observations in the
  limited review report.
- The fourth quarter is often derived as full-year audited figures less nine-month reviewed figures, so year-end
  audit adjustments land in it.
- Consolidated and standalone results can tell different stories when subsidiaries are loss-making.

## Red flags
- A modified limited review report or an emphasis of matter.
- Fourth-quarter figures that absorb large adjustments reversing earlier quarters' profits.
- Frequent regrouping of prior-period figures.
- Results filed late, or board meetings for results repeatedly postponed.

## Benign explanations
- Seasonal businesses with structurally weak or strong quarters.
- Timing of festive demand (Diwali falling in different quarters in different years).

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Stock exchange announcements on NSE and BSE (PDF and XBRL), the company's investor relations page, and the
  accompanying investor presentation and earnings call transcript.

## Questions for management
- What drove the change against the same quarter last year and against the previous quarter?
- Are there any year-end audit adjustments expected to affect the fourth quarter?
