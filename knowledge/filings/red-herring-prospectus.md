# Reading a DRHP or RHP

## What it measures
A Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) is filed with SEBI for observations; after incorporating them, the company
files a Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) with the Registrar of Companies before the offer opens. The RHP contains
everything except the final price and the number of shares, which are fixed after book building. Both follow
Schedule VI of the SEBI ICDR Regulations.

Sections an analyst relies on:
- **Offer Document Summary** — business, objects, shareholding, summary financials, litigation, related party
  transactions.
- **Risk Factors** — internal and external risks; read the first twenty in full.
- **The Offer / Objects of the Offer** — fresh issue and offer for sale; use of proceeds.
- **Basis for Offer Price** — EPS, P/E, return on net worth, peers, KPIs, weighted average cost of acquisition.
- **Capital Structure** — share capital history, promoter holding, lock-in.
- **Our Business / Industry Overview** — business model and the industry report commissioned for the offer.
- **Restated Financial Information** — usually three years (plus a stub period when the latest year end is more
  than six months old), restated for consistent accounting policies, with an examination report by the auditor.
- **Management's Discussion and Analysis** — explanation of results and cash flows.
- **Financial Indebtedness**, **Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments**, **Government and Other
  Approvals**, **Our Promoters and Promoter Group**, **Our Management**.

## How to read it
- Eligibility route: ICDR Regulation 6(1) requires, among other conditions, net tangible assets, an average
  operating profit of at least ₹15 crore over the preceding three years with operating profit in each of them, and
  positive net worth. Issuers that do not meet these conditions list under Regulation 6(2), which requires at least
  75% of the offer to be allocated to qualified institutional buyers.
- Under Regulation 6(1), at least 35% of the offer is reserved for retail individual investors.
- Read the restated financial information first for trend and cash conversion, then risk factors to see which
  risks the company itself acknowledges, then objects, valuation and capital structure.
- Compare the DRHP with the RHP: changes to the peer set, objects, selling shareholders or risk factors between the
  two are informative.
- The industry report is commissioned and paid for by the company; treat market size claims with care.

## Red flags
- Negative operating cash flow or losses in the restated period, with a large offer for sale.
- Significant restatement adjustments or qualifications in past audit reports.
- High general corporate purposes or vague objects.
- Material litigation or regulatory actions against promoters.
- Heavy dependence on related parties, a few customers or a single product.

## Benign explanations
- Growth companies under Regulation 6(2) with transparent KPIs and improving unit economics.
- Private equity exits from mature, profitable companies.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- SEBI's website (public issues section) for DRHPs and RHPs; stock exchange websites for RHPs, price band
  announcements, anchor allocation and basis of allotment.

## Questions for management
- Why is the company raising capital now, and why are selling shareholders exiting?
- What changed between the DRHP and the RHP?
