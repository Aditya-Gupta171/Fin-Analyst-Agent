# Reading the statement of profit and loss (Schedule III, Ind AS)

## What it measures
Division II of Schedule III to the Companies Act sets the format for companies following Ind AS: revenue from
operations and other income make up total income; expenses are presented by nature (cost of materials consumed,
purchases of stock-in-trade, changes in inventories, employee benefits expense, finance costs, depreciation and
amortisation, other expenses); then exceptional items, profit before tax, tax expense (current and deferred),
profit for the period, other comprehensive income, total comprehensive income, and earnings per share.
Non-banking financial companies use Division III, and banks and insurers use their regulators' formats.

## How to read it
- Revenue from operations may include "other operating revenue" such as export incentives or scrap sales; check the
  note.
- Cost of materials plus purchases plus change in inventories approximates cost of goods sold. A negative change in
  inventories means stock built up during the period.
- EBITDA is not a Schedule III line; derive it consistently (revenue from operations less total expenses, adding
  back finance costs and depreciation).
- Other comprehensive income holds remeasurement of defined benefit plans, fair value changes on certain equity
  investments, cash flow hedge movements and translation differences. Large OCI losses do not reduce profit but do
  reduce equity.
- In consolidated statements, look for share of profit of associates and joint ventures and the split of profit
  between owners and non-controlling interests.

## Red flags
- Revenue presentation changes (gross to net or the reverse) without restating comparatives.
- Large or growing other income and exceptional items relative to PBT.
- Persistent OCI losses on hedges or investments.

## Benign explanations
- Presentation changes mandated by amendments to Schedule III, applied with restated comparatives.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Annual report and restated financial information; quarterly results use a condensed version of the same
  structure.

## Questions for management
- What is included in other operating revenue and other expenses?
