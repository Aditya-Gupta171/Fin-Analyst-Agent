# Reading the balance sheet (Schedule III, Ind AS)

## What it measures
Schedule III Division II presents non-current assets (property, plant and equipment, capital work-in-progress,
right-of-use assets, goodwill, intangibles, financial assets, deferred tax assets), current assets (inventories,
financial assets such as investments, trade receivables, cash and bank balances, loans), equity (equity share
capital and other equity) and liabilities, each split into non-current and current (borrowings, lease
liabilities, trade payables, other financial liabilities, provisions).

## How to read it
- The 2021 amendments to Schedule III added disclosures analysts should use: ageing schedules for trade receivables,
  trade payables and capital work-in-progress; details of promoter shareholding changes; title deeds not held in
  the company's name; loans to promoters, directors and related parties repayable on demand; and key ratios with
  explanations for changes above 25%.
- "Other financial assets" and "other current assets" can hide advances, deposits and unbilled revenue; read the
  notes.
- Right-of-use assets and lease liabilities (Ind AS 116) bring leases onto the balance sheet.
- Current maturities of long-term borrowings are included in current borrowings.
- Goodwill and intangibles from acquisitions depend on impairment testing assumptions.

## Red flags
- Loans and advances to related parties, especially repayable on demand.
- Large capital advances or deposits with no clear counterparty explanation.
- Goodwill that has not been impaired despite losses in the acquired business.
- Rising "other current assets" without explanation.

## Benign explanations
- Balance sheet growth following an acquisition or equity raise, with clear notes.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Annual report balance sheet and notes; the half-yearly statement of assets and liabilities filed with results.

## Questions for management
- What do other financial assets and other current assets consist of?
- What assumptions support the carrying value of goodwill?
