# Sector primer — NBFCs

## What it measures
Non-banking financial companies lend without taking public deposits (with few exceptions) and fund themselves
through bank loans, bonds, commercial paper and securitisation. Most apply Ind AS and present under Division III of
Schedule III. RBI's scale-based regulation groups them into base, middle, upper and top layers with increasing
requirements. Key measures:
- **Asset quality:** Stage 2 and Stage 3 assets under the Ind AS 109 expected credit loss model, reconciled with RBI's
  income recognition and asset classification norms; provision coverage on Stage 3.
- **Capital:** CRAR (15% minimum for middle- and upper-layer NBFCs) and tier 1 capital.
- **Liabilities:** asset-liability maturity mismatch, share of short-term funding such as commercial paper, cost of
  borrowing, concentration of lenders.
- **Profitability:** net interest margin, operating expenses to assets, credit cost, return on assets.

## How to read it
- Funding is the classic NBFC failure point: short-term borrowing funding long-term loans collapses when markets
  freeze.
- ECL model assumptions (probability of default, loss given default, management overlays) are judgemental; watch
  overlays being released to support profit.
- Rapid assets-under-management growth seasons slowly; delinquencies lag disbursements by several quarters.
- Co-lending and direct assignment move loans off balance sheet but not all of the risk.

## Red flags
- Negative cumulative mismatch in short-term maturity buckets.
- Stage 2 assets rising quickly while Stage 3 stays low.
- Release of management overlays while collection efficiency falls.
- Loans to promoter group entities or large exposures to real estate developers.

## Benign explanations
- Stage 3 increases in a disclosed, provided-for portfolio being run down.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Annual report notes on ECL staging, provisions, capital adequacy and asset-liability maturity pattern; quarterly
  results notes; credit rating rationales.

## Questions for management
- What is the maturity mismatch in the up-to-one-year buckets and how is it funded?
- How have ECL overlays changed and why?
