# Sector primer — infrastructure and EPC contractors

## What it measures
EPC contractors execute multi-year projects, mostly for government agencies and public sector undertakings.
Developers build and operate assets under BOT, toll, annuity or hybrid annuity model (HAM) concessions through
special purpose vehicles. Key measures:
- Order book and book-to-bill (order book relative to annual revenue), order inflow, execution rate.
- Working capital: receivables, contract assets (unbilled revenue), retention money held by clients, mobilisation
  advances received.
- Bank guarantees and letters of credit used, disclosed in contingent liabilities.
- Arbitration awards and claims, and their recognition.
- Leverage at the parent and in project SPVs.

## How to read it
- Revenue is recognised over time using the input method; margin depends on estimates of total project cost.
- Government customers pay slowly but rarely default; the risk is cash flow timing and disputes.
- Claims and arbitration awards should generally not be recognised as revenue until realisation is highly probable;
  aggressive recognition inflates profit.
- Parent company guarantees and investments in loss-making SPVs are the main hidden risks.

## Red flags
- Contract assets and receivables growing faster than revenue for several years.
- Revenue from claims or arbitration awards recognised before collection.
- Order book dominated by a few slow-moving projects or by projects stuck on land acquisition.
- Rising bank guarantee usage relative to net worth.
- Liquidated damages and cost overruns disclosed in notes but not provided.

## Benign explanations
- Temporarily high receivables at year end when government budget releases are delayed, recovered in following
  quarters.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Revenue and contract balance notes, contingent liabilities (guarantees, claims), segment notes, investments in
  subsidiaries and joint ventures; order book in investor presentations and MD&A.

## Questions for management
- How much of the order book is executable in the next twelve months, and how much is stalled?
- What claims are recognised in revenue or receivables pending arbitration?
