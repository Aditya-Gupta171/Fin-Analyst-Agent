# Sector primer — pharmaceuticals and healthcare

## What it measures
Indian pharmaceutical companies sell branded generics in India, generics in the US and other regulated markets, and
active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) or contract manufacturing services. Hospitals earn from occupied beds and
procedures. Key measures:
- Revenue split by domestic formulations, US generics, emerging markets, APIs and contract development and
  manufacturing.
- US regulatory status of plants: inspection outcomes (Form 483 observations, classification as no action,
  voluntary action or official action indicated), warning letters and import alerts.
- R&D spend as a share of revenue, and how much is capitalised.
- For hospitals: average revenue per occupied bed, occupancy, average length of stay.

## How to read it
- US generic prices erode every year; growth depends on new launches, including limited-competition products.
- A warning letter or import alert on a key plant can stop US shipments from it for a long period.
- In India, medicines on the National List of Essential Medicines are price-controlled under the Drugs (Prices
  Control) Order, limiting price increases.
- Capitalising development costs under Ind AS 38 raises current profit; impairment later reverses it.
- For hospitals, new beds are loss-making for a few years; judge mature and new units separately.

## Red flags
- Official action indicated classification or warning letters at plants contributing significant US revenue.
- Capitalised R&D growing faster than expensed R&D.
- One or two limited-competition US products contributing a large share of profit.
- Hospitals with high receivables from government schemes or insurers.

## Benign explanations
- Temporary US revenue dips from planned plant remediation disclosed in advance.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- MD&A and investor presentations (business segments, regulatory status), stock exchange disclosures of inspection
  outcomes, intangible assets notes, risk factors in offer documents.

## Questions for management
- What is the regulatory status of each plant supplying the US market?
- How much development expenditure was capitalised and what is its impairment risk?
