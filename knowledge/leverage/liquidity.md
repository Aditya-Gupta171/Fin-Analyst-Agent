# Short-term liquidity — current and quick ratios

## What it measures
The current ratio divides current assets by current liabilities; the quick ratio excludes inventories, which may
take time to sell. Both ask whether the company can meet obligations falling due within twelve months.

## How to read it
- A current ratio below 1 means current liabilities exceed current assets. For most manufacturers and service
  companies that is a liquidity concern; for consumer staples and retailers with negative working capital it can be
  a strength.
- Quality matters more than the ratio: receivables that are overdue or inventory that is slow-moving overstate
  liquidity.
- Current borrowings include current maturities of long-term debt; a large maturity falling due next year can push
  the ratio below 1 temporarily.
- Unused sanctioned credit limits and cash equivalents give a truer view of liquidity headroom.

## Red flags
- Current ratio below 1 combined with negative operating cash flow.
- A going concern paragraph or material uncertainty related to going concern in the auditor's report.
- Classification of long-term borrowings as current because of covenant breaches (Ind AS 1 requires this when the
  lender can demand repayment).
- CARO reporting of defaults in repayment of loans, or quarterly stock statements submitted to banks that do not
  agree with the books.

## Benign explanations
- Negative working capital business models collecting cash from customers before paying suppliers.
- A scheduled bullet repayment already refinanced after the balance sheet date.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Balance sheet classification of current assets and liabilities; Schedule III additional regulatory information
  includes the current ratio.
- Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020 reporting on defaults, use of short-term funds for long-term purposes, and
  reconciliation of quarterly returns filed with banks.
- Liquidity risk section of the financial risk management note.

## Questions for management
- What undrawn committed facilities are available?
- Have any loan covenants been breached or waived?
