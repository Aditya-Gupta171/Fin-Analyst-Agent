# Interest coverage and debt service

## What it measures
Interest coverage is operating EBIT divided by finance costs: how many times operating profit covers the interest
bill. Finance cost to revenue shows how much of each rupee of sales goes to lenders.

## How to read it
- Below about 2.5x a modest fall in operating profit threatens debt service; below 1x operations do not cover
  interest at all.
- Use operating EBIT, excluding other income, so treasury gains do not mask a weak core business.
- Under Ind AS 23, borrowing costs on qualifying assets under construction are capitalised rather than expensed.
  Reported finance costs can understate the true interest burden during a capex cycle; add back capitalised
  interest, disclosed in the notes, for a truer coverage figure.
- Under Ind AS 116, lease interest is part of finance costs, so coverage for lease-heavy businesses changed after
  adoption without any change in the business.
- In quarterly results, coverage can swing with seasonality; compare with the same quarter last year.

## Red flags
- Coverage falling in consecutive periods while borrowings rise.
- Large or growing capitalised interest on projects that are delayed.
- Interest paid in the cash flow statement well above finance costs in the profit and loss statement.
- Coverage below 1x in a quarter outside the company's normal seasonal pattern.

## Benign explanations
- A temporary dip in operating profit from a disclosed one-time event.
- Newly commissioned assets carrying a full interest charge before reaching normal utilisation.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Finance costs note (interest on borrowings, lease interest, other borrowing costs).
- Property, plant and equipment or capital work-in-progress note for borrowing costs capitalised.
- Schedule III additional regulatory information includes the debt service coverage and interest service coverage
  ratios with explanations for changes above 25%.

## Questions for management
- How much interest was capitalised during the period and on which projects?
- What is the average cost of borrowing and how has it changed?
