# Contingent liabilities and commitments

## What it measures
Under Ind AS 37 a provision is recognised when an outflow is probable and can be estimated reliably. Obligations
that are only possible, or cannot be measured reliably, are disclosed as contingent liabilities instead of being
recorded. Typical items: tax demands under appeal (income tax, GST, customs), claims against the company not
acknowledged as debt, bank guarantees and corporate guarantees given for others, and disputed statutory dues.

## How to read it
- Scale them against net worth. Contingent liabilities above half of equity mean an adverse outcome on a
  meaningful portion could impair the balance sheet.
- Separate guarantees for the company's own obligations (performance guarantees in EPC contracts) from guarantees
  for related parties, which are closer to real exposure.
- Tax disputes are common in India and many are resolved in the company's favour, but look at the forum and
  history: demands confirmed at appellate levels are more likely to crystallise.
- Track movement year to year and whether amounts move from contingent liabilities into provisions.

## Red flags
- Contingent liabilities growing faster than net worth, or exceeding it.
- Corporate guarantees for promoter group entities or loans of related parties.
- Large claims in arbitration by customers or lenders.
- Items disclosed as contingent in one year and suddenly written off as exceptional losses in the next.

## Benign explanations
- Performance bank guarantees that are a normal part of contracting with government customers.
- Legacy tax disputes on issues where courts have repeatedly ruled in taxpayers' favour.

## Where to find it in Indian filings
- Contingent liabilities and commitments note in the financial statements; provisions note for amounts recognised.
- In offer documents: "Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments" (tax proceedings and material civil
  litigation per the materiality policy) and the restated contingent liabilities statement.

## Questions for management
- What are the largest contingent liabilities, at which forum are they pending, and what is the expected outcome?
- Are any guarantees given for entities outside the group or for promoter group companies?
